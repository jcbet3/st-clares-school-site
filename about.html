About our school

A caring, faith-centred day and boarding school in Kiptere.

Our school

Education is a partnership between school and home. We welcome families who want a caring, faith-centred start for their children.

St. Clare’s is a mixed school offering both day and boarding places. Children learn in a caring community just off the Kericho-Sondu highway, near St. Francis Catholic Church.

Parents are welcome to visit and meet the teachers before applying.

What we stand for

Mission

We commit ourselves to provide quality teaching to enable all pupils to be knowledgeable in all areas and to achieve a positive, friendly atmosphere in which pupils can be successful.

Vision

To foster a partnership among teachers, parents and pupils which will lead to shared responsibilities for quality learning.

Learning and school life

Learning is matched to each stage of a child’s growth.

Lower Primary

Grades 1 to 3

Fluent reading, number sense and good study habits.

Upper Primary

Grades 4 to 6

Deeper learning in science, languages and creative arts.

Day school

Home each evening

Learners come in each morning and return to their families after school.

Boarding

Boarders

Boarding places are available. Ask the office about space and what to bring.
